# Personal expense tracking

The owner's recorded income and expenses.

## Language

**Category**:
A label from the owner's editable list used to group income or expenses for reporting. Examples include utilities, restaurants, software, and groceries.

**Payee**:
The person or organization on the other side of an expense or income, such as a retailer, a service provider or an employer. Each payee has one default category.
_Avoid_: Counterparty, Vendor, Payer, Merchant

**Default category**:
The category applied through a payee unless an entry has an explicit override. Changing it also changes the classification of past entries that used the previous default.

**Category override**:
A transaction-specific category choice that takes precedence over its payee's default category.

**Payee alias**:
An alternative name for the same payee, kept when the owner merges two payees. For example, “AMZN MKTP” may be an alias of “Amazon.”

**Account**:
A place the owner's money sits and is paid from, such as N26, Revolut, Cash or an Indian bank account. Each transaction names one.

**Spending**:
The total of expenses during the reporting period. Refunds, internal transfers and savings do not reduce or add to it.

**Refund**:
Money returned for an earlier expense, recorded as income. It does not reduce spending.

**Reporting date**:
The date selected by the owner to determine which reporting period contains a transaction. It need not be the bank posting date or the date the entry was created.

**Internal transfer**:
A movement of money between accounts owned by the same person, such as sending euros to the owner's Indian bank account. It is neither income nor an expense.
_Avoid_: Remittance (as a separate concept)
