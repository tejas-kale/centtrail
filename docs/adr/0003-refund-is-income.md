# A refund is income and does not reduce spending

A refund is recorded as ordinary income with its own payee and category, rather than reducing spending in the original purchase's category. This reverses the earlier rule, under which a refund was linked to its purchase and netted against that category in the month received. The owner chose simplicity of capture over net category figures: refunds need no link, and the transaction kinds shrink to expense, income and transfer. The consequence is that spending figures stay gross: a returned €100 purchase shows €100 spending and €100 income in the same month.
