# 03: Refund is income; drop repayment

**What to build:** A refund is recorded as ordinary income and no longer reduces spending. The refund-to-purchase link and the Repayment kind are removed. Spending is the total of expenses in the period, excluding the Savings category, transfers and trashed entries.
